## Diagnostic hurdle

The signed deviation is `D = RAWBEN - BENFIX`; `FSBEN` remains only the engine-computable `formula_benefit` feature. Stage 1 is `|D| > $0.50`. Stage 2 is fit and evaluated only among those stage-1 positives; rare official errors with `|D| <= $0.50` retain their diagnostic label but are outside the stage-2 and magnitude fits.

Training weighted/unweighted prevalence is 37.64%/36.72% for stage 1 and 10.62%/10.18% for the official-error label. FY2024 weighted/unweighted prevalence is 41.01%/39.79% and 13.39%/12.12%, respectively.
