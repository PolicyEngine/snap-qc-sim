Administrative quality control data as an oracle

Verifying encoded SNAP rules and simulating payment error under cost sharing. The QC files as a verification oracle — 6,081 in-scope fiscal 2024 cases across seven states reproduced exactly, zero dollar tolerance — a distributional error model reported with its validation failures, and the fiscal 2025 rates landing where the sampling-noise mechanism says realized years should: 18 of 53 jurisdictions changed cost-share tiers, only 7 of those changes beyond two-year sampling noise. This page embeds the revision-6 manuscript (updated 2026-08-12), which carries four rounds of adversarial review, archived unedited, and adds the engine-adoption accounting bounds priced through the penalty formula.
